About StayEase

StayEase Hotel Booking System is a modern hotel management platform developed using Python and Flask. It allows customers to explore rooms, make bookings, and manage reservations with a simple and user-friendly interface.

Our goal is to provide a fast, secure, and comfortable booking experience while helping hotel administrators efficiently manage rooms and customer details.
